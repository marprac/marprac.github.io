02 Research

Bachelor’s thesis
Bachelor’s thesis · Artificial intelligence Solving Tasks from the ARC-AGI-1 Corpus Using Artificial Intelligence A hybrid LLM system for abstract reasoning from just a few examples. 2025

University of Ljubljana · FRI · 2025

My bachelor’s thesis investigates abstract reasoning on ARC-AGI-1 tasks. The system uses a large language model and DSPy to generate rules and actions, test them on grids, and iteratively improve them using difference reports. With GPT-5-mini, it achieved 61.5% accuracy on 60 training tasks and 43% on 100 public evaluation tasks.

Supervisor: Jure Žabkar.
